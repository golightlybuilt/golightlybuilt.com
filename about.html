Golightly Construction & Foundation Repair is led by Colton Golightly and serves property owners in Lamar County, Texas. The work is guided by a simple belief: good projects start with listening, honest communication, and a plan that makes sense for the property.

Whether you have a foundation concern, a construction project, or questions about what to do next, you can start with a conversation. Colton will hear what is happening, discuss your goals, and help you understand the next step. Every property is different, so project recommendations and pricing are confirmed individually.
